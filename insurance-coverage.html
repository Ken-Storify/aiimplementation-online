Is Your AI Covered? A Self-Check

Five questions. No signup, nothing saved anywhere, it just runs in your browser. At the end, you'll get a plain read on where you likely stand, and the exact email to send your broker.

Does your organization currently use any AI tool in customer-facing operations, documentation, or decision support?

Do you know whether your general liability carrier has filed an AI exclusion endorsement (such as ISO CG 40 47, CG 40 48, or CG 35 08) in your state?

Have you reviewed your AI vendor contracts for indemnification language covering AI-caused harm?

Does your current policy explicitly address claims arising from an AI monitoring, documentation, or decision-support failure?

Has your organization experienced, or come close to, a loss or claim connected to an AI tool?
