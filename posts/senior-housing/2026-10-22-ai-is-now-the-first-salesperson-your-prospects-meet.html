From the October 22, 2026 LITT Briefing

AI Is Now the First Salesperson Your Prospects Meet

Prospective residents and families are asking ChatGPT and Gemini about senior living communities before they ever call. A session at this week's 2026 NIC Fall Conference in Chicago on October 22 is built around live demos of how these tools describe a community using reviews, public data, and available content (NIC session listing). Separately, U.S. News & World Report announced a partnership with, and investment in, Candid, which brings conversational voice and text feedback tools to operators (U.S. News release).

The leadership move: Make one executive responsible for how AI describes your communities, audit the answers this week, and publish a consent and data-use notice before deploying any AI tool that collects resident or family feedback.
